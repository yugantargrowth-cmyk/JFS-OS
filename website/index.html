Our Story

A Decade of Transforming Ahmedabad's Spaces

Founded in 2014 by Ashutosh Jangid, Jangid Furniture Studio began with a simple belief — that every home and workspace deserves furniture that is both beautiful and built to last. What started as a small workshop in Ahmedabad has grown into a trusted name across Gujarat.

Over the past decade, we have completed 500+ projects ranging from modular kitchens in residential homes to full office buildouts for corporate clients. Our commitment to quality materials, honest pricing, and timely delivery has earned us the loyalty of over 450 happy clients.

We specialize in custom wood, PVC, aluminium, and glass works — handling everything from design consultation to final installation under one roof.

What We Offer

Our Services

01

Modular Kitchen

We design and install custom modular kitchens that blend style with smart storage. Whether you need an L-shape, U-shape, parallel, or island kitchen, we tailor every unit to your exact space and lifestyle requirements.

  • L-shape, U-shape, parallel & island layouts
  • High-quality shutters: matte, gloss, acrylic, membrane
  • Soft-close hinges and drawer channels
  • Custom countertop: granite, quartz, or laminate
  • Overhead units, base units & tall units
02

Wardrobes & Storage

Maximize your space with our fully customized wardrobe solutions. From compact bedroom wardrobes to spacious walk-in closets, every unit is designed to provide optimal storage while enhancing the room's aesthetics.

  • Sliding, hinged & folding door systems
  • Internal fittings: drawers, pull-outs, tie racks
  • Loft units, study tables & TV panels
  • Premium laminate, veneer & PVC finishes
  • Floor-to-ceiling designs for maximum storage
03

Aluminium & Glass Partitions

Transform open spaces into functional zones with our sleek aluminium and glass partition systems. Perfect for offices, showrooms, and commercial interiors seeking a modern, professional look.

  • Full-height and mid-height glass partitions
  • Single & double-glazed systems
  • Aluminium framed and frameless glass options
  • Office cabins, conference room enclosures
  • Toughened safety glass with varied frosting options
04

Office & Showroom Furniture

We create custom furniture solutions for offices, showrooms, clinics, and retail spaces. From executive desks to full workstation setups, every piece is crafted to support productivity and leave a lasting impression.

  • Custom workstations and cluster seating
  • Reception desks and front office counters
  • Cabin furniture, director desks & credenzas
  • Retail display shelving & showroom fixtures
  • Conference tables and lounge furniture
05

PVC Cabinets & Panels

Our PVC-based cabinet and panel solutions offer 100% waterproof, termite-proof, and maintenance-free alternatives to traditional wood. Ideal for bathrooms, utility areas, and humid environments.

  • 100% waterproof & termite-proof construction
  • Bathroom vanity units and mirror cabinets
  • Utility room and balcony storage units
  • PVC wall panels with textured & glossy options
  • Easy to clean, long-lasting & eco-friendly
